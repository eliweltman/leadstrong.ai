How Much Do Lead Generation Services Cost?

On this page

Pricing for lead generation services is one of the least transparent areas in marketing. Providers rarely publish rates. Proposals look different from every agency. And the gap between a $1,500/month retainer and a $15,000/month program is not always obvious from the outside.

This guide gives you a clear-eyed look at what lead generation actually costs, what drives the variation, and how to think about budget relative to the outcomes you are trying to achieve.

The Range Is Wide, and the Reasons Are Legitimate

Lead generation services can cost anywhere from a few hundred dollars a month for a basic campaign management service to $50,000 or more per month for a comprehensive, multi-channel program run by a specialist agency. The variation reflects genuine differences in scope, expertise, and what is included.

A low-cost service typically covers one channel with minimal strategy, limited creative production, and basic reporting. A mid-market program covers multiple channels, includes active campaign management, regular optimization, and detailed performance reporting. A premium program involves senior strategists, full creative production, and a dedicated team working across all relevant channels.

The Main Pricing Models

Monthly Retainer

The most common model for ongoing lead generation programs. You pay a fixed monthly fee for a defined scope of work. This provides cost predictability and is the right model when you need sustained, consistent activity rather than a one-off project.

Performance-Based

Some providers tie their fee to results - a flat fee per lead generated, or a percentage of revenue from leads they produce. This aligns incentives but can create pressure to maximize lead volume over lead quality, so it needs to be set up carefully with clear qualification criteria.

Project-Based

A fixed fee for a defined piece of work - a website audit, a campaign build, a strategy development project. This works well for one-time needs but does not suit ongoing program management.

What Drives the Price Up

Channels involved. A LinkedIn-only program costs less than a program running LinkedIn, Google, content, outbound prospecting, and CRO simultaneously. Each channel adds expertise requirements and management time.

Markets served. Running campaigns in Canada, the US, and the UK simultaneously requires market-specific targeting, localised creative, and knowledge of each market's buyer behavior and competitive landscape. That is more complex and more expensive than a single-market program.

Seniority of the team. The difference between a program managed by a junior account executive and one with a senior strategist involved is significant - both in the quality of strategic decisions and in the long-term performance trajectory of the program.

Ad spend management. Most agencies charge separately for managing your paid media budget - typically 15 to 20 percent of ad spend or a flat management fee. Confirm whether this is included or additional.

Realistic Budget Ranges for Growing Businesses

For businesses in Canada, the US, and the UK that are serious about building pipeline, here are realistic budget expectations.

Entry-level programs covering one or two channels with basic management: $1,500 to $4,000 per month in agency fees. These work for very small businesses with simple needs but often lack the scope to produce meaningful pipeline for growing businesses.

Mid-market programs covering multiple channels with active management and strategy: $5,000 to $15,000 per month. This is the range where most growing businesses with genuine pipeline goals should be operating.

Premium full-service programs with senior team involvement and comprehensive channel coverage: $15,000 to $40,000 per month. Appropriate for businesses with significant growth ambitions and the revenue to justify the investment.

Ad spend is typically additional to these figures. Most programs in Canada, the US, and the UK require a minimum of $3,000 to $5,000 per month in media spend to generate meaningful results from paid channels.

How to Think About ROI

The question is not "how much do lead generation services cost" in isolation. It is "what does a qualified lead - and ultimately a customer - need to be worth for this investment to make sense."

If your average customer is worth $30,000 in annual revenue and your close rate from qualified leads is 20 percent, a qualified lead is worth approximately $6,000 to your business. A program generating ten qualified leads per month at a total cost of $10,000 (agency fees plus ad spend) has a positive ROI before you consider the lifetime value of those customers.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much do lead generation services cost?

Reputable programs for growing businesses in Canada, the US, and the UK typically range from $5,000 to $15,000 per month in agency fees for a multi-channel program, plus media spend. Entry-level services cost less but often lack the scope to produce meaningful pipeline. Premium programs with senior team involvement and full channel coverage can range from $15,000 to $40,000 per month.

Is ad spend included in lead generation pricing?

Usually not. Most agencies charge their management fee separately from ad spend. Always confirm what is included and ask for a breakdown of agency fees versus media investment.

How do I know if I am getting good value?

Measure cost per qualified lead, not cost per lead or cost per click. A program that generates 100 leads at $50 each is worse value than one that generates 20 qualified leads at $250 each if the qualified ones are the ones that actually convert.
