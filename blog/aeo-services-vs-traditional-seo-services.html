AEO Services vs Traditional SEO Services

On this page

When businesses start exploring AEO, one of the first questions they ask is whether it replaces their existing SEO investment or sits alongside it. The short answer is alongside - but the specifics of how these two types of service differ are worth understanding before you decide how to invest.

What Traditional SEO Services Include

Traditional SEO services focus on improving a website's ranking in Google's link-based search results. The typical scope includes keyword research to identify which terms your target audience searches for, technical SEO audits and fixes that affect how search engines crawl and index your site, on-page optimization of content and metadata, link building to improve domain authority, and ranking and traffic reporting.

Traditional SEO is a well-established discipline with clear success metrics. When your pages rank higher for relevant keywords, they generate more organic traffic, which generates more leads and revenue. The causal chain is measurable.

What AEO Services Include

AEO services focus on positioning your content to be cited by AI search tools. The typical scope includes AI search content audit and restructuring for direct-answer formatting, structured data implementation with FAQPage, Article, and HowTo schema, topical cluster architecture to build the domain-level authority AI tools assess, content brief development with AEO requirements built in, and AI citation monitoring and reporting.

AEO has a less linear causal chain than traditional SEO because AI citations do not always generate measurable clicks. The value is partly in brand visibility, partly in authority building, and partly in capturing the share of search behavior that is now happening in AI tools rather than traditional search.

Where They Overlap

The good news is that the two approaches share significant common ground. Well-structured, high-quality content that directly answers questions is good for both traditional SEO and AEO. Strong internal linking and topic cluster architecture benefits both. Factually accurate content with good source attribution builds credibility for both search engines and AI tools.

Where they diverge is in the specific technical requirements (structured data for AEO, technical crawl health for SEO), in the metric frameworks (AI citation monitoring for AEO, ranking and traffic for SEO), and in the content formatting priorities (direct-answer upfront for AEO, keyword-optimized headings and metadata for SEO).

Why Growing Businesses Need Both

Businesses in Canada, the US, and the UK that depend on search for lead generation cannot afford to optimize for only one of these channels in 2026. Traditional search still drives significant traffic, and that traffic is still worth capturing. AI search is becoming an increasingly important discovery channel, and the businesses that build AI citation authority now are building an advantage that will compound over time.

LeadStrong's content programs are designed to serve both channels - building content that ranks in traditional search and is cited in AI search, without treating them as competing priorities.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between AEO services and SEO services?

Traditional SEO services optimize content for ranking in link-based search results. AEO services optimize content to be cited by AI search tools. The technical requirements and success metrics differ, but the underlying content quality principles overlap significantly.

Can one agency provide both SEO and AEO services effectively?

Yes, and ideally they should - because the two approaches inform and reinforce each other. LeadStrong delivers integrated content programs that address both traditional and AI search visibility as a unified strategy.

Should I prioritize SEO or AEO if my budget is limited?

Start with strong foundational SEO - technical health, keyword-targeted content, on-page optimization. Then layer AEO requirements onto your content production process, which adds cost incrementally rather than requiring a separate budget. LeadStrong can help you sequence this investment efficiently.
