Best Schema Types for AEO

On this page

Structured data - the code added to web pages to tell search engines what type of content they contain - is one of the most actionable elements of AEO implementation. Certain schema types are more directly relevant to AI search visibility than others. Here is a practical guide to the ones that matter most and why.

FAQPage Schema

FAQPage schema is the most directly relevant schema type for AEO. It explicitly marks up a section of your page as containing a list of questions and answers - exactly the format AI search tools are designed to surface.

When implemented correctly, FAQPage schema makes your Q&A content machine-readable, improving the likelihood that AI tools extract your answers when responding to relevant queries. It also makes your content eligible for Google's FAQ rich results in traditional search.

How to implement it: mark up each question-answer pair with the appropriate schema properties (name for the question, acceptedAnswer for the answer). Most CMS platforms support this through SEO plugins.

Article Schema

Article schema identifies your content as an article and provides structured information about it - title, author, publication date, and other metadata. For AI tools that assess content freshness and authorship, this metadata helps establish the recency and credibility of the content.

Use Article schema on most editorial content. NewsArticle is available for timely news content; BlogPosting is appropriate for blog format. The distinction is less important than having some version of Article schema implemented.

HowTo Schema

HowTo schema marks up step-by-step instructional content - processes, guides, and tutorials. When your content explains how to do something, HowTo schema makes each step explicitly machine-readable.

This is particularly relevant for AEO because "how do I..." queries are among the most common questions people put to AI search tools. If your content provides step-by-step guidance and that structure is marked up with HowTo schema, you are optimizing for one of the highest-volume AEO query types.

DefinedTerm and Speakable Schema

DefinedTerm schema marks up glossary or definition content - useful for articles that define technical terms or industry concepts. Speakable schema identifies content suitable for audio responses (originally for voice assistants, but increasingly relevant for AI tools that synthesise spoken responses).

These are more niche than FAQPage and Article schema but worth implementing in relevant content types.

Organization Schema

Organization schema on your homepage and About page provides authoritative information about your business - name, location, contact information, and social profiles. This helps AI tools correctly identify your business when synthesising responses that reference your brand or services.

It is not AEO in the direct content-citation sense, but it supports brand visibility in AI-generated responses and helps ensure your business information is accurate across AI tools.

Implementation Priority

For most businesses, the right implementation order is: FAQPage schema first (highest AEO impact), Article schema second (broad applicability across your content), then HowTo schema where relevant, and Organization schema on key brand pages.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which schema type is most important for AEO?

FAQPage schema has the most direct AEO impact because it explicitly marks up question-and-answer content for machine extraction. Article schema is broadly important for establishing content type and metadata.

Is implementing schema markup technically difficult?

Not for most CMS platforms. WordPress plugins like Rank Math and Yoast SEO support FAQPage and Article schema through user interfaces that require no coding. On custom platforms, implementation typically requires a developer but is usually a few hours of work.

Does schema markup guarantee AI search citations?

No. Schema markup is a strong signal that improves the likelihood of citation, but AI tools make their own assessments of content quality and relevance. Schema is most effective when combined with high-quality, well-structured content.
