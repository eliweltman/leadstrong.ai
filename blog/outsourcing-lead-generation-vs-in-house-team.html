Outsourcing Lead Generation vs Building an In-House Team

On this page

This is one of the most consequential marketing decisions a growing business makes, and it rarely gets the structured analysis it deserves. Both approaches can work. Both have real limitations. And the right choice depends on factors specific to your business - your growth stage, your budget, your existing team, and how quickly you need results.

The Case for Building In-House

An in-house lead generation team has real advantages. Internal marketers develop deep knowledge of your business, your customers, and your competitive positioning over time. They are available for the quick pivots and informal conversations that external agencies are not always plugged into. And if you are building a business where marketing is a core strategic function, internal talent may be the right long-term investment.

The cost picture is also worth understanding clearly. A mid-level marketing hire in Canada or the UK costs $70,000 to $100,000 per year in salary, before benefits, tools, and management overhead. But a senior marketing hire who can genuinely own multi-channel lead generation - paid media, content, SEO, outbound - typically commands $100,000 to $140,000 per year. And one person cannot do all of these things to a high standard simultaneously.

The Limitations of Going In-House

Channel expertise is the core challenge. LinkedIn advertising, Google Ads, technical SEO, conversion rate optimization, and structured outbound prospecting are each specialist disciplines. A generalist marketing hire can cover some of them adequately. They are unlikely to be excellent across all of them, which means some parts of your program will underperform.

Ramp time is also a real cost. A new hire takes three to six months to become fully effective in most roles. During that period you are paying full salary while pipeline contribution is minimal.

The Case for Outsourcing

The primary advantage of working with a specialist agency is immediate access to expertise across every relevant channel. You do not need to hire a paid media specialist, an SEO strategist, and a CRO expert separately - you get all of those capabilities through one engagement.

Speed to results is also significantly faster. An agency can have campaigns live within weeks, drawing on proven approaches from previous programs rather than starting from scratch. And the management overhead sits with the agency rather than with your leadership team.

The Limitations of Outsourcing

Agencies do not have the same depth of institutional knowledge as internal team members. Building that context takes time and requires active involvement from your team. An agency relationship where the client is disengaged rarely produces good results - it requires regular communication, clear feedback, and genuine collaboration to work well.

Cost predictability can also be a concern. A good agency engagement represents a meaningful monthly investment. For businesses at an early stage, this may not be the right model until there is enough revenue to justify it.

The Hybrid Approach Most Businesses Land On

For most growing businesses, the most effective structure is a hybrid. Internal resource - a marketing manager or head of growth - owns the strategy, the relationship with the agency, and the business knowledge. The agency executes across the specialist channels, brings external expertise and channel experience, and takes the operational management of campaigns off the internal team's plate.

This combination typically produces better results than either approach alone, at a lower cost than building a full specialist in-house team.

Frequently Asked Questions

Should I outsource lead generation or hire in-house?

For most growing businesses, a hybrid works best - an internal marketing leader who owns strategy and business context, supported by a specialist agency that executes across paid, content, and outbound channels. Pure in-house execution requires significant headcount to cover all relevant expertise. Pure agency execution works best with strong internal involvement.

How much does it cost to build an in-house lead generation team?

A single senior hire capable of running multi-channel lead generation costs $100,000 to $140,000 per year in Canada or the UK. A full team covering paid media, content, SEO, and outbound would typically require three to four people and $300,000 to $500,000 per year.

What are the advantages of outsourcing lead generation?

Immediate access to specialist expertise across multiple channels, faster time to results, lower total cost than building an equivalent in-house team, and no hiring or ramp-time risk.
