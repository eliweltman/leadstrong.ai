Top Lead Generation Strategies That Actually Work

On this page

Marketing is full of lead generation strategies that sound plausible and perform poorly. This article focuses on the approaches that consistently deliver results for B2B businesses in Canada, the US, and the UK when implemented properly - not the theory, but what actually works and why.

Build Your Ideal Customer Profile Before You Build Anything Else

This appears on every lead generation list and gets skipped just as often. It is listed first here because everything downstream of it - channel selection, ad creative, landing page copy, content topics, outbound messaging - is only as good as the ICP that informs it.

A useful ICP is not a vague persona description. It is a precise, evidenced picture of the companies and individuals that get the most value from what you offer, built from your actual best customers. Their industry and company size. Their job title and decision-making authority. The specific problem they had before they found you. The language they used to describe that problem. What made them decide to buy. This detail is what makes campaigns land.

Run Paid Search for High-Intent Keywords

When someone types "B2B lead generation agency Canada" or "outsourced CFO services London," they are ready to have a conversation. Paid search campaigns targeting these high-intent queries put your business in front of buyers at the best possible moment.

The strategy is not complicated. The execution is. Effective Google Search campaigns require careful keyword selection, strong match type discipline, well-structured ad groups, landing pages that are tightly relevant to the query, and ongoing bid management. Done well, this is one of the highest-returning lead generation investments available. Done poorly, it burns budget quickly.

Use LinkedIn to Reach Specific Decision-Makers

No other platform lets you advertise to a VP of Finance at a 200-person software company in Toronto or a Head of Operations at a manufacturing business in the Midlands with the precision LinkedIn does. For B2B businesses with a clearly defined buyer profile, that targeting capability is extremely valuable.

The most effective LinkedIn campaigns in 2026 combine sponsored content - typically a short-form video or a single image with a clear value proposition - with LinkedIn Lead Gen Forms that capture contact details without the prospect leaving the platform. The Lead Gen Form format consistently outperforms campaigns that drive to a landing page for B2B audiences because it reduces friction at the conversion point.

Create Content That Answers the Questions Your Buyers Are Actually Asking

The temptation with content marketing is to write about what you find interesting rather than what your buyers are searching for. The businesses generating consistent organic pipeline from content do the opposite.

They identify the specific questions their ideal customers are asking at each stage of the buying journey - awareness questions like "what causes inventory stockouts," consideration questions like "how to choose inventory management software," and decision questions like "best inventory software for distributors." They create articles that answer these questions thoroughly and are optimized both for search engines and for AI tools that increasingly synthesise content into direct answers.

Run Structured Outbound Prospecting

Outbound is not spam. Structured outbound prospecting - with a defined target account list, personalized multi-touch sequences, and genuine relevance to the prospect's context - generates meaningful pipeline for businesses with a clear ICP and a defined value proposition.

The key word is structured. A good outbound program involves researching each target account, writing personalized initial messages that reference something specific to the prospect's situation, following up with additional value rather than just a nudge, and managing the sequence in a proper sales engagement tool like Apollo, Outreach, or HubSpot.

Optimize Your Highest-Traffic Pages for Conversion

Traffic that does not convert is wasted investment. For many businesses, their website has enough visitors to generate significantly more leads than it does - but the pages visitors land on are not designed to capture that intent.

Reviewing your highest-traffic pages and improving the clarity of the offer, the relevance of the CTA, and the friction in the lead capture process can double or triple lead volume from existing traffic without spending an additional pound or dollar on acquisition.

Frequently Asked Questions

What lead generation strategies work best for B2B businesses?

The most consistently effective strategies are high-intent paid search, LinkedIn advertising with Lead Gen Forms, content marketing optimized for buyer intent keywords, structured outbound prospecting, and conversion rate optimization of high-traffic pages. The right combination depends on your specific ICP and the markets you are targeting.

How long do these strategies take to produce results?

Paid search and LinkedIn can generate leads within weeks of launch. Content and SEO take three to six months to build meaningful pipeline. Outbound prospecting can generate conversations within days if the targeting and messaging are right.

Do these strategies work in Canada, the US, and the UK?

Yes, with market-specific adjustments to targeting, messaging, and platform emphasis. LeadStrong designs programs specifically for businesses operating in these three markets.
